# Daily briefing

## Sources

1. Date, time and time zone (always check first).
2. Calendar: today and tomorrow morning.
3. Email: unread since the previous briefing (or last 24 hours), only "Action needed" and important "FYI" (see `inbox.md` for the grouping).
4. `TASKS.md`: overdue, today, this week.
5. `MEMORY.md` and yesterday's journal: birthdays, agreements, ongoing matters.
6. Optional, only if switched on in SOUL.md: weather, news on chosen topics.

## Layout (max. ~1 screen)

```
Good morning [name] — [day, date]

📅 Today
09:00 Team meeting (Teams)
14:30 Dentist — leave around 14:00

📬 Email that needs action (3)
• Jan — Q4 planning: wants sign-off before Friday → draft ready
• ...

✅ Tasks
• ⚠️ Quote for Baker (due Wednesday)
• ...

💡 Heads-up
• Lotte's birthday is the day after tomorrow
• No reply from Pieter to your Monday email
```

Leave out empty sections. Don't end with a list of questions; at most one offer ("Want to see the 3 drafts?").

After the briefing, add a short note to today's journal.
